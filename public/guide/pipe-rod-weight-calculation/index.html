Pipe & Rod Weight Calculation

The weight of a bar is its cross-sectional area times its length and material density. A solid rod uses the full section; a pipe uses the annular section.

⚙ Open the Hydraulic Calculator →

Weight Formula

Mass

m = ρ × A × L

Solid rod section

A = π × d² / 4

Pipe section

A = π × (D² − d²) / 4

D: outer diameter, d: inner diameter. Steel density ρ ≈ 7.85 kg/dm³.

Units

Worked Example

Given: Weight per metre for a steel solid rod Ø40 mm and a steel pipe Ø60/Ø50 mm.

1) Solid rod Ø40: A = π×40²/4 = 12.57 cm² → m = 9.86 kg/m

2) Pipe Ø60/Ø50: A = π×(60²−50²)/4 = 8.64 cm² → m = 6.78 kg/m

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the density of steel?

About 7.85 kg/dm³ (7850 kg/m³). Aluminium ≈ 2.70; stainless steel ≈ 8.00 kg/dm³.

What is the difference between pipe and solid rod?

A solid rod uses the full circular section (π×d²/4); a pipe uses the annular section with the bore removed (π×(D²−d²)/4).

How do I get the total weight?

Multiply the weight per metre (kg/m) by the length of the part (m).
