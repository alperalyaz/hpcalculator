Gear Pump Selection

To pick the right gear pump you need three things: the required flow, the motor speed, and the working pressure. From these the required displacement is found.

⚙ Open the Hydraulic Calculator →

Required Displacement

Vg = Q × 1000 / (n × ηv)

Q: required flow (L/min), n: speed (rpm), ηv: volumetric efficiency (≈0.90–0.95), Vg: cc/rev

Worked Example

Given: Required flow Q = 40 L/min, motor speed n = 1450 rpm, volumetric efficiency ηv = 0.92.

1) Displacement: Vg = 40 × 1000 / (1450 × 0.92) = ≈ 30 cc/rev

2) Choose the nearest standard displacement (e.g. 30 cc/rev)

3) Verify the pump's pressure class covers the working pressure

Frequently Asked Questions

How is pump displacement selected?

From the required flow and speed via Vg = Q×1000/(n×ηv), then round up to the nearest standard displacement.

Why does the pressure class matter?

Every pump has a maximum continuous/intermittent working pressure. Your system pressure must stay below that limit.

How does speed affect the pump?

Flow is proportional to speed. The same displacement gives more flow at higher rpm, but you must respect the manufacturer's min/max speed limits.
