Pressure Unit Conversion (bar · psi · MPa)

In hydraulics, pressure appears as bar, psi, MPa and kgf/cm². The factors below let you convert between all of them.

Conversion Factors (1 bar)

1 bar = 100,000 Pa = 100 kPa = 0.1 MPa
1 bar = 14.5038 psi
1 bar = 1.01972 kgf/cm² ≈ 0.987 atm

Worked Example

Given: Two common conversions.

1) 250 bar in psi → 250 × 14.5038 = 3,626 psi

2) 3000 psi in bar → 3000 ÷ 14.5038 = 206.8 bar

3) 250 bar = 25 MPa = 254.9 kgf/cm²

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the ratio between bar and psi?

1 bar = 14.5038 psi. Multiply to go from bar to psi, divide to go from psi to bar.

How does bar relate to MPa?

1 bar = 0.1 MPa, so 10 bar = 1 MPa. MPa is the SI unit and is common in engineering calculations.

Is kgf/cm² almost the same as bar?

Yes, very close: 1 bar = 1.0197 kgf/cm². In practice they are often treated as equal, but a precise calculation should use the factor.
